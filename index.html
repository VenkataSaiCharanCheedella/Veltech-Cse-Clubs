Important Recruitment Instructions

Please read and acknowledge the mandatory instructions before continuing:

  1. Leadership positions are only open for 2nd and 3rd Year students.
  2. 4th Year students are NOT eligible for leadership positions.
  3. Club Member positions are open for all students.
  4. Applicants should possess at least one of the following:
    • Technical Skills
    • Leadership
    • Creativity
    • Responsibility
    • Genuine Interest
  5. Club Heads are expected to conduct meetings and organize events.
  6. Vice Heads assist Club Heads.
  7. Members are expected to actively participate.
  8. Filling this application does not guarantee selection.
  9. Inactive students may be removed from clubs.
  10. Decisions made by the CSE Clubs Council are final.
